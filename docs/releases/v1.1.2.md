# WatchLog 1.1.2: three fixes to RabbitMQ's page

Found on the live demo right after 1.1.1, when two things had happened within the hour.

## What's fixed

- **Markers on a chart no longer write over each other.** With two or more events in range
  (a consumer OOM-killed, then another), their labels take turns on two rows above the plot,
  each with its time first.
- **A client that restarted and is connected again** is no longer linked from a finding or a
  queue's probable cause as a lost consumer (Kafka's findings too); it stays on the charts as a
  neutral marker.
- **Deliver behind publish** says "below the 5/s published" instead of quoting the hour's peak
  (often a backlog draining after a consumer came back).
