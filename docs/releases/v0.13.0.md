# WatchLog 0.13.0: certificates and scheduled jobs, easy to find

WatchLog has found your TLS certificates and scheduled jobs on its own since 0.10. Now they
are where you look, and they show time: when each certificate expires, when each job ran.

## What's new

- **Their own pages:** **Certificates** and **Scheduled jobs** are in the sidebar, under a new
  **Running here** group with Applications; Events, What changed, Service map and Egress sit
  under **Investigate**. Each entry has a badge when something needs a look: amber for a
  certificate expiring within 14 days, red for an expired one, one under 3 days, a certificate
  problem, or a job failing, missed or slow. The collapsed sidebar shows a dot. Applications is
  the services list again. Old links (`/applications/certificates`,
  `/applications/scheduled-jobs`) open the new pages, filters included.
- **Running here on the Overview:** under the tiles, three cards: Services, Certificates (the
  next 90 days as a strip, then the three soonest) and Scheduled jobs (the three that need a
  look first, each with its last 12 runs). On a Server fleet in the server's words: "nginx :443
  on app-1", "systemd timer on worker-1", "cron on db-1 · 0 4 * * *"; and a certificate whose
  renewal job fails says so: "certbot.timer is failing".
- **When they expire:** the Certificates page shows every certificate as a dot on the day it
  expires, over 30 days, 90 days or a year, with the usual renewal window shaded and the later
  ones counted at the end. A label opens the certificate.
- **Runs:** the Scheduled jobs page gives each job a lane of its runs over 6 hours, 24 hours or
  7 days: fine, failed, slow, missed, and the next runs ahead. A mark opens the job at that run.
  The table's filters apply to the lanes. Both pages remember the range you picked.
- **Found on its own, said once:** the first time WatchLog finds certificates or jobs in a
  cluster or fleet, the Overview says so, with the most urgent one. It doesn't come back once
  dismissed.
- **Search** (⌘K) shows certificates and jobs with a status line: "expires in 9 days · Ingress
  shop/api".
- **All clusters and fleets (Pro):** two tiles, certificates expiring within 14 days and
  scheduled jobs failing, and a Certificates and a Jobs column; the API and MCP
  `get_all_clusters` carry the same counts.
- An empty Certificates or Scheduled jobs page says how they are found on your platform and
  which agents are too old to look, with a link to update them.

## Fixes

- "Has no process left" fired when a one-off job finished normally: a systemd oneshot unit's
  processes, or a cron job's. It now fires only for a group whose service still runs (servers
  and Kubernetes).
- A crontab line on a server where the cron daemon isn't running was reported as having missed
  its run (Debian's php `sessionclean`, `e2scrub_all`); it is shown as disabled, with no next
  run, and never alerts.
- The Kafka collector probed the brokers' exporter ports five times an hour even when none
  answered; it now backs off up to once a day.
- Egress alerts said "new since the last message" on rules that send no messages (the read-only
  demo's).
- Certificate and job alerts' messages link to their own pages.
- A certificate sent without names that don't match read as a name mismatch.

## Upgrading

No manual steps: one migration adds a column for the once-only notice. Agents 0.13.0 carry the
cron and Kafka fixes; older agents keep working.
