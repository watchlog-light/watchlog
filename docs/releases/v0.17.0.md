# WatchLog 0.17.0: Wi-Fi, storage, recording and UPSes

The network module now reads the Wi-Fi a MikroTik router manages, a QNAP NAS and its iSCSI LUNs,
Dahua video recorders, and UPSes. Above all it tells you when a camera system **stops
recording**: which recorder, which signal failed, since when. Like the rest of the network
module, it is part of WatchLog Cloud Pro and self-hosted Enterprise (a licence key that lists
`network`).

## What's new

- **Network → Wireless:** the access points CAPsMAN manages (both RouterOS generations, read
  over the router's own read-only connection): which are up, their radios and channels, the
  switch port each hangs off, clients over the day; the traffic with its usual band; every
  client with its access point, band, signal and rates, filterable and searchable. Mesh links
  between access points are drawn dotted on the map. Access points are free.
- **Network → Storage:**
  - **Recording**, one card per recorder: recording or not, from three signals shown side by
    side (the recorder's own state and events, its iSCSI disk, its writes toward the NAS against
    their usual rate); cameras online; which LUN it writes to, the free space and the days of
    recording left at the current rate; its cameras.
  - **The NAS:** disks with temperature and SMART, volumes and RAID state, LUNs and who is
    connected to each.
  - **iSCSI sessions:** recorder to LUN, since when, throughput and the switch path between them,
    which the map's new **iSCSI paths** layer draws.
  - **UPSes:** status, charge, runtime, load, voltages, the battery's state, and which of your
    servers and nodes it feeds: when it goes on battery, their Events say so.
- **Network → Overview** has a Recording card: one line per recorder.
- **Read-only, as before:** CAPsMAN through print and GET, the QNAP and UPSes over SNMP, Dahua
  recorders with a fixed list of HTTP GET calls and their event stream (HTTP digest, a dedicated
  user, HTTPS pinned on first contact). Profiles for QNAP, UPS-MIB and APC PowerNet-MIB are data,
  like the others.
- **Seventeen new default alerts:** an access point disconnected, one with no clients when it
  usually has them, many weak clients, 2.4 GHz channel overlap; a NAS disk's SMART, a hot disk,
  RAID degraded or rebuilding, a volume or LUN over 90%, an iSCSI session lost; a recorder not
  recording, its iSCSI disk lost, its storage full within 3 days, cameras offline (one alert per
  recorder); a UPS on battery, with under 10 minutes left, needing a new battery, or overloaded.
  A recorder going "not recording" waits 5 minutes and names the signal; a camera going offline
  never makes it.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do: the migrations run on start. The new kinds need the integrations collector of
0.17.0 where they are polled. Setup for each kind (the users to create, the ports to open) is in
the network devices docs.
