# WatchLog 1.4.1: MongoDB's operations by type, and what the live demo showed

Found on the live demo within the hour of 1.4.0.

## What's new

- **MongoDB's operations by type are back**: queries, inserts, updates, deletes, get-mores and
  commands a second, on the Overview and the Operations tab, with each kind's mean latency
  (reads, writes, commands) beside them. 1.4.0 had left the chart out of the new Overview.
- **The shapes table says operation and returned**, not statement and rows.
- **Clearer numbers in MongoDB's findings:** the documents each scan reads are written short
  ("197 k"), as in the table, and the client sending the scans says "N · scans" in the flow.
- **Redis's key sampler keeps a small instance's prefixes fresh**: it starts a new pass as soon
  as its budget allows (every minute or two for a few thousand keys) instead of once every 10
  minutes, within the same bound of 20,000 names a window.
- **The busiest collections leave out MongoDB's own `$cmd` namespaces.**

Nothing to do; no migration. Update the integrations collector with the rest.
