# WatchLog 0.9.7

A small follow-up to 0.9.6 for WatchLog Cloud. Nothing changes for self-hosted Community
installs.

## Fixed

- **"Learning resumed on <date>" was missing for a gap older than 0.9.6.** The learning page
  of What changed says when learning went on after a gap (the organization was not on Pro, or
  no data came). 0.9.6 only noted that when a gap happened after the update, so an
  organization whose gap was before it saw the corrected day and date, but not the line. It is
  now worked out from the hours the worker has looked at
  ([ADR-217, amended](https://github.com/watchlog-light/watchlog/blob/v0.9.7/docs/DECISIONS.md)).

## Upgrading

No manual steps and no database migration. Agents are unchanged: 0.9.2 agents need no
upgrade.
