# WatchLog 0.9.8

Two fixes, for self-hosted installs and WatchLog Cloud alike.

**Self-hosted with a Telegram channel: upgrading is recommended.** Since 0.1.0 a Telegram alert
could be sent up to 8 times within about an hour, and its "resolved" message was never sent.
0.9.8 fixes both. Slack, email and webhook channels were not affected.

## Fixed

- **Telegram: a delivered message was recorded as a failure, and sent again.** WatchLog read
  only the first 500 characters of a provider's answer, and Telegram's answer to a sent message
  is almost always longer (always, when the alert has its link to WatchLog), so its
  `"ok": true` was not seen. For such an alert since 0.1.0 this meant: it was delivered, recorded
  as failed and sent again, up to 8 times in all within about an hour; its
  "resolved" message was never sent; "Send test" said "Test failed: Telegram sendMessage
  answered 200: {…" although the test was in the chat; and the channel read "Failing". Slack,
  email and webhook channels were not affected. The whole answer is read now. Updating settles
  the messages still waiting for a retry, so no further copy goes out, and clears the false
  failure from the channel
  ([ADR-218](https://github.com/watchlog-light/watchlog/blob/v0.9.8/docs/DECISIONS.md)).
- **Channel errors are short and say what to do.** "Telegram refused the message: the bot was
  blocked in the chat (403). Unblock the bot in Telegram, or connect another chat." The
  provider's raw answer is no longer shown, stored or logged: Telegram's named the chat and its
  people. A long error wraps inside its card, and the card's header agrees with the row
  ("1 failing", not "1 connected" over a failing channel).
- **After a rate limit (429) the next try waits as long as the provider asks.**
- **Settings › Users: Revoke and Remove on an invite now ask first, and the page shows what
  happened.** Revoking a pending invite or removing an expired one applied at once, and after
  that, after removing a member, changing a role or handing ownership over, the list did not
  change until the page was reloaded and no message appeared (the change itself was made). Each
  now opens a dialog, the row goes at once and a message says what was done
  ([ADR-219](https://github.com/watchlog-light/watchlog/blob/v0.9.8/docs/DECISIONS.md)).
- **The same dialog before other things that delete or take access away:** deleting an alert
  channel, signing another device out, signing out everywhere else, unlinking GitHub or Google
  (Cloud), resetting a rule to its default, and removing an application's stored access.

## Upgrading

No manual steps. One database migration runs by itself: it marks Telegram messages that were
delivered as sent and takes the providers' raw answers out of stored error texts. Agents are
unchanged: 0.9.2 agents need no upgrade.
