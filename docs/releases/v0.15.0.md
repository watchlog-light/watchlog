# WatchLog 0.15.0: the Enterprise licence key

A self-hosted WatchLog can now have every Pro feature. Add an Enterprise licence key and What
changed and All clusters and fleets turn on, for every organisation on the installation. The
key is checked on your own server, offline, so it works air-gapped too.

## What's new

- **Settings › License** (Owners): paste a key and see at once who it is for, the units used of
  allowed, the features, when it ends, its id and where it came from. Or set `WATCHLOG_LICENSE`
  (or `WATCHLOG_LICENSE_FILE`) where WatchLog runs; the environment wins over a pasted key.
- **Pro on your own server:** What changed starts learning when the key is added; All clusters
  and fleets opens; the MCP tools `get_what_changed` and `get_all_clusters` appear. Docker
  Compose runs What changed's worker with `COMPOSE_PROFILES=enterprise`; Helm with
  `license.key` and `anomalyWorker.enabled=true`.
- **Units:** Kubernetes nodes plus servers, as the highest daily count of the last 7 days. Over
  the limit, Owners see "58 of 50 units. Your licence covers 50; contact us for a larger one."
  Monitoring never stops; after 30 days over the limit, licensed features take on no new places,
  and everything they already cover keeps working.
- **Expiry:** a banner and one email to Owners 30 days before the end; 14 days of grace after
  it; then licensed features turn off and their pages say "Your licence ended on …". Nothing
  is deleted: a new key turns them back on with their data.
- **The About line** in the account menu says "WatchLog Community" or "WatchLog Enterprise,
  licensed to Acme". `GET /api/v1/health` adds `edition` and `licensed`.
- **`ee/` for Enterprise code:** under its own source-available licence (`ee/LICENSE`), loaded
  only with a key that lists the module's feature. The core builds and runs without it, and CI
  checks that on every push.

Read [Enterprise licence](https://watchlog.io/docs/license/) for how to add a key, units,
expiry and grace.

## Upgrading

No manual steps; one migration. Without a key nothing changes: a self-hosted install stays
Community, with everything it had. WatchLog Cloud ignores keys. Agents need no update.
