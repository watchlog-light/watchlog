# WatchLog 1.0.0

WatchLog now takes in the numbers your own software reports: orders, failed payments, checkout
time, jobs waiting, the last good backup. They sit on the workload or server they belong to,
go on dashboards you build from widgets, and can be pinned and alerted on, with no query
language anywhere. Your CI can tell WatchLog about a deploy. And every dropdown in the app is
now one searchable Combobox.

Upgrading needs no manual step. Agents older than 1.0.0 keep working; scraping an endpoint
needs the 1.0.0 integrations collector.

## Added

- **Your metrics** ([docs](https://github.com/watchlog-light/watchlog/blob/v1.0.0/docs/site/your-metrics.md),
  ADR-236). Three ways in: annotate a pod (`watchlog.io/scrape: "true"`) or give an address on a
  server and the collector reads its `/metrics` every 30 seconds; point Prometheus remote write
  at WatchLog with a write-only token; or push a value with one HTTP call. "Find endpoints for
  me" lists the ports that already answer with metrics.
- **Each metric as its type reads.** A counter as a rate, a histogram as its p95 (with p50 and
  p99), a timestamp as "3 h ago", a gauge as it is. Types and units come from what the sender
  says and from the name, and can be corrected. "Split by" one label shows the five highest
  values and the rest.
- **Limits that say what they refused.** Series per organisation and per source, and 100 values
  of one label on one metric; over a limit, new series are refused, known ones keep flowing,
  and the Sources table names what was dropped and why ("too many values for `user_id`").
  WatchLog's own monitoring is never affected. A self-hosted installation sets its cap with
  `backend.ownMetricSeries` (`WATCHLOG_OWN_METRIC_SERIES`, 20,000 by default).
- **Pin to a page.** Up to 6 of its own metrics on a workload's page or a server's page.
- **Dashboards you build** (ADR-237). The organisation's, on a 12-column grid. A widget is a
  metric, a shape (Number, Line, Area, Bars), at most one label to split by and a size. Edit
  with a side panel and a live preview; drag or use the keyboard to move and resize; "Done"
  saves. There is no query language, no variables and no formulas, and WatchLog's own pages stay
  as they are.
- **Alerts on your metrics.** Above or below a value, not reported for a while, a timestamp
  older than a while; the rule shows the sentence it will say before you save it. They are
  ordinary alert rules: under Alerts with the rest, on the workload or server they name.
- **Watch with What changed (Pro)** (ADR-238). A watched metric learns its usual week: its
  usual range is shown with its value and as a band on its chart, "outside its usual range"
  can be alerted on, and it is one more series in What changed's list and story.
- **Change events from outside** (ADR-238). `POST /api/v1/events` from CI or a script: a deploy,
  a config change, a migration. It appears in Events, as a line on charts, under "Changed
  around then" and in probable cause, with the token's name. Examples for curl, GitHub Actions
  and GitLab CI are in the docs.

## Changed

- **One searchable Combobox everywhere** (ADR-239). Every dropdown filters as you type and
  highlights what matched; long lists are windowed; the list opens upward when there is no
  room, is never clipped by a dialog, and is a sheet on a phone. The last native selects (a
  user's role, the logs panel, the egress filters, the network devices' filters and forms) are
  gone.
- **Dashboards are no longer a non-goal, within a limit** (SPEC §2.5): they exist for your own
  metrics only, built from widgets.

## Fixed

- The setup checklist said "1 servers".
- An alert about a server's systemd service could name its place "Service undefined/…".
- A recorder's side panel on the network map and a device's header showed facts their kind
  doesn't report ("Uptime –").
- "Days of recording left" read "0 days" after a restart; it is worked out from the last
  hour's average and says "calculating" until it has one.
