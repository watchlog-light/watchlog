# WatchLog 0.12.1: fewer, truer alerts

A short list of alerts, each telling a different story.

## What's new

- **One "New external destination" alert per workload:** a workload that talks to many new
  names gets one alert, "updater started talking to 24 new destinations", that lists each name
  with its first connection. It resolves after 24 hours without traffic to any of them, and
  repeats only when names joined since its last message. Alerts already open are folded into
  one per source on upgrade.
- **One story per workload:** "has 0 of 1 replicas ready" waits under "is crash-looping" for
  the same workload, and a crash loop waits under the OOM kills that cause it (Kubernetes pods,
  a server's services and process groups). They count as held back, send no message, and show
  on the other alert's page.
- **What changed lists a series once**, its strongest current change, with "and 3 more changes".
- **Seed files can retire rules:** `retiredRules` deletes rules an earlier seed made, with
  their alerts ([configuration](../site/configuration.md#seed-file)).

## Fixes

- Alerts on a service's metrics (Redis, PostgreSQL, Nginx, Traefik…) and on a pod's metrics
  named their subject "Node" with no name; they name the service or the pod, on the page, in
  messages and through MCP.
- A custom rule on pod restarts counted the total, which never goes down, so its alerts never
  resolved; it counts the last 15 minutes, and existing rules move over on upgrade.
- Applications no longer suggests monitoring WatchLog's own database.
- Kafka's logo was invisible in the light theme.
- The read-only demo no longer lists "sends alerts nowhere".

## Upgrading

No manual steps: two migrations fold the open egress alerts and move restart rules. Agents
don't need an update for this release.
