# WatchLog 1.0.3: "Add a source" that a first-time user can follow

A patch release for Your metrics. Someone who has never heard of Prometheus sends their first
number in under two minutes; someone with an exporter adds it in three clicks.

## What's changed

- **Add a source starts with a question: "Where is your number?"**
  ([docs](https://github.com/watchlog-light/watchlog/blob/v1.0.3/docs/site/your-metrics.md#your-first-metric-in-two-minutes),
  ADR-242). In a script, a cron job, CI or your own code: push a value. An app or an exporter
  with a `/metrics` page: expose it. You already run Prometheus: send from it. Push opens
  first, unless WatchLog found a `/metrics` page here or you chose another way last time.
- **Push a value is complete on one screen.** A token is made right there and shown once; you
  say what the number belongs to, its name and its value; the command is filled in with your
  installation's address in curl, Bash, Node.js, Python, PHP and Go. "Send a test value for me"
  sends one without a terminal. Then the page waits, and says **Received `orders_total` = 1**
  when it arrives, with Open it, Put it on a dashboard and Alert on it. After two minutes with
  nothing, it says what to check.
- **Expose /metrics says what it is, and never dead-ends.** A sample of what a `/metrics` page
  looks like; **Test** reads an address once before it is scraped and says what it found
  (how many series, or: nothing is listening, a web page, it asks for a password, too slow,
  too big); "Find endpoints for me" says which ports it tried; and when there is nothing to
  read, three ways forward: push instead, a few lines with your language's Prometheus library,
  or an exporter. In a cluster a workload's port is added with no restart, and the annotation
  is given as one `kubectl patch`.
- **Limits on sending**
  ([docs](https://github.com/watchlog-light/watchlog/blob/v1.0.3/docs/site/your-metrics.md#limits-on-sending)).
  Besides a token's own rate, all of an organisation's tokens together now have a ceiling
  (`429` with `Retry-After`), and when the backend is strained, calls with your own metrics are
  answered `503` for a moment while the agents' data keeps being taken: WatchLog's own
  monitoring is never the thing that gives way. A push carries at most 500 values and 128 KB.
  An organisation has at most 20 own-metrics tokens; tokens made before keep working.
- **A series is said in plain words** wherever its number appears: one line on a chart, not a
  call. `orders_total` is 1 series however often you send it.

## What's fixed

- The address field's placeholder was shown in capitals and read like a value.
- A refused or unknown token no longer costs a database query on every call.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do; no migration. Agents need no update. **Test** on Add a source needs the 1.0.3
collector (`install.sh --upgrade` on a server, the chart's new version in a cluster); with an
older one it says the collector didn't answer, and everything else works as before. The three
new settings (`WATCHLOG_SHED_…`, `backend.shed` in the Helm chart) have safe defaults.
