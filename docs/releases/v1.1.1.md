# WatchLog 1.1.1: RabbitMQ's page agrees with itself

The RabbitMQ page from 1.1.0, with every number telling the same story and the layout after its
design. No new data and nothing to change on your side.

## What's fixed

- **Consumers** read the same everywhere: the flow, the Consumers tile, the queue rows, the
  status and the health checks all come from one source at one instant. A client whose pod or
  container restarted and is connected again shows as running, "restarted", not gone, and its
  marker on the charts stays neutral.
- **How busy** the consumers are was RabbitMQ's consumer utilisation as is; utilisation 1.0
  means they keep up. It now shows 1 − utilisation: "100% busy" no longer means idle.
- **Deliver** warns only when it is below publish and ready messages are growing, not when it is
  lower than the hour's peak (often a backlog draining).
- **Memory** shows the same number with the same rounding in the tile and the health check.
- The broker is never listed as its own client, and long-lived publishers are counted; the
  Overview no longer repeats the connected list the Clients tab owns.
- **Queues that need a look** lists only queues with a finding; with none, one line says so.
  "All 1 queues" reads "All queues".
- Rates use a window of at least two steps, so a steady publisher is a steady line at 6 h and
  24 h. A marker on a chart always shows its time and what happened.
- A queue with ready messages and no consumers is a finding even when it isn't growing yet
  (a warning; critical when it grows).
- The queue page's busy chart showed a raw key for its note.

## Layout

- The message flow: the number at 26 px with its unit beside it, up to three names per card
  that are never cut, the rate above each arrow, the red border where messages stop, equal
  heights.
- The tiles: sentence-case labels, 26 px values, a line under every value ("steady", "2 an
  hour ago").
- The health checks in a 380 px column beside the queues that need a look.
- "What WatchLog sees" is one line when everything is connected.
- "Dropped, returned and dead-lettered" moved to the Queues tab and is one line when nothing
  was.
- No empty bar when a queue has no ready messages; counts with thousands separators.
