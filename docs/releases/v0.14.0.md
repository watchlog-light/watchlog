# WatchLog 0.14.0: What changed, after the learning week

What changed used to give you a chart and a list once its learning week was over. Now the page
tells you what happened: what is outside right now, the episode in one sentence, what likely
caused it, how it spread, and what each series did against its usual band.

## What's new

- **A live map:** every series WatchLog scores is one square, green while it's inside its
  usual range, faint amber near the edge, amber when it's outside. Squares are grouped by
  workload on Kubernetes and by server and its services on a fleet (`postgres · db-1`), and
  groups with something outside come first. It refreshes every minute; the header says
  "Watching 251 series · checked 12 s ago".
- **The episode as a story:** "shop/backend's CPU went to 14× usual 2 minutes after the rollout
  of backend; shop/postgres's CPU then went to 3.4× usual." It comes from fixed templates, not
  an AI. Under it, a green **Likely cause** box or a grey **No clear cause** box that names the
  checks that found something. ‹ 2 of 3 › steps through the episodes.
- **How it spread:** the change, then each series in the order it moved, with the minutes
  between. Arrows only follow calls on the service map or a shared node or server; anything
  else is listed as "moved at the same time; no calls between them".
- **What moved:** each series with a sparkline against its usual band, the moment it left
  marked, and a "14× usual" or "÷1.5 usual" chip. Sort by First to move or Furthest out.
- **Why we think so:** every check, what changed around then (rollouts and scaling; on servers
  also scheduled runs, service starts and restarts, and image changes), and Open the evidence,
  Copy as text, Ask your AI.
- **The chart, last:** a count axis, the episodes shaded (click one to select it) and the
  changes as labelled markers.
- **When everything is inside:** "All 251 series are inside their usual range for this hour",
  and the last 7 days' episodes as cards.
- **Hide WatchLog's own parts:** on by default, so the agent and WatchLog's own services don't
  crowd your map. Your browser remembers the choice.
- **API and MCP:** `/what-changed` gives the episodes, the story and the changes; there are
  two new routes, `/what-changed/live` and `/what-changed/recent`. MCP `get_what_changed` adds
  the story, the chain and what changed around then.

## Fixes

- **Scheduled jobs:** a systemd timer's next run already includes its random delay, so it now
  shows as an exact time instead of "around".
- **Docs:** the install guide has a step-by-step section for a Docker server.

## Upgrading

No manual steps. One migration adds a small table for the live map. The learning week,
scoring and the alert rules are unchanged, and agents need no update.
