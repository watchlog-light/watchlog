# WatchLog 0.16.0: network devices

WatchLog now sees the network your servers and nodes sit on: every switch and router, what is
connected to what, which switch port each server or node is on, how busy each link is, and
what broke. It is part of WatchLog Cloud Pro and self-hosted Enterprise (a licence key that
lists `network`); Cloud Free and Community show what it does under **Network** in the sidebar.

## What's new

- **Network → Overview:** devices up, ports with problems, links over 80%, the oldest
  firmware; the map; what needs attention; every device with its CPU, memory, temperature,
  ports up, firmware and the place that polls it.
- **The map**, drawn from CDP, LLDP and MNDP (or the MAC tables when a switch speaks none of
  them): bands from the Internet down to the servers and nodes, status rings, links thicker
  the faster they are, moving dashes for traffic, amber when busy or erroring, red and dashed
  when down. Click a device to light up its path to the Internet and open its details; click a
  link for both ends, its use each way, errors and how it was learnt. Owners and Admins can
  drag the layout; it is kept for the organisation.
- **A device's page:** its front panel with each port by state (stacks: one panel per
  member), a port's traffic against its speed, its neighbour or what's behind it, VLANs and
  PoE; CPU, memory and temperature; fans and power supplies; SFP optics against their own
  thresholds; events; for MikroTik, the RouterOS version and whether an update is out.
- **Add devices:** choose the cluster or server that polls them, a credential profile (SNMP v3
  by default, v2c, or a RouterOS user), addresses, a /24 range or neighbour discovery, and
  add what was found. The setup commands for Cisco IOS, NX-OS and RouterOS are shown with your
  poller's address filled in.
- **Read-only, from inside your network:** the integrations collector you already run polls
  the devices: SNMP with GET, GETNEXT and GETBULK only (the client can't send a SET), MikroTik
  over its REST API or API-SSL with the router's certificate pinned. Vendor support is data:
  one YAML profile each for generic SNMP, Cisco IOS, Cisco NX-OS and MikroTik.
- **Linked to your servers and nodes:** a server's or node's page shows its switch port
  (`sw-access-2 · Gi1/0/14 · 1 Gb/s`). A port going down, an uplink dropping packets or a switch
  rebooting shows in that place's Events, in What changed and in its probable cause.
- **Thirteen default alerts** (device unreachable, rebooted, uplink down, port errors, speed
  dropped, link busy, CPU, memory, temperature, fan or power supply, SFP optics, stack member
  lost, a RouterOS certificate changed), editable and silenceable like any other, one story
  per device.
- **Counting:** a network device is one unit, like a node or a server; Wi-Fi access points a
  controller manages are free.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do: the migrations run on start, and the agents start reporting their network
cards. Network devices need the integrations collector of 0.16.0 where they are polled.
