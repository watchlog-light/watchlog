# WatchLog 1.3.1: the tour's proof on a phone

Two fixes found within minutes of 1.3.0, walking the guided tour on the live demo.

## What's fixed

- **On a phone, the tour's proof step shows the chart its words name.** When the alert page had
  What changed rows above the chart, the step scrolled to them and the chart sat under the
  tour's sheet. On a phone it now lights the chart alone; on a desktop it still lights both.
- **The alert page's What changed "and N more" link** is a 44 px target on phones (it was 20 px).

## Upgrading

Nothing to do; no migration. The web image only; the backend, agent and collector are the same
as 1.3.0's.
