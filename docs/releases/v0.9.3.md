# WatchLog 0.9.3

**WatchLog Cloud Pro's price, before billing starts.** Pro is now **$3 per node or server a
month**, with a **$15 minimum** that covers the first 5. Nobody has paid the earlier price:
billing had not started ([ADR-215](https://github.com/watchlog-light/watchlog/blob/v0.9.3/docs/DECISIONS.md)).
Nothing changes for self-hosted installs.

## What changed

- **Cloud Pro:**
  - monthly: $3 per node or server, on the 95th percentile of the month's hourly counts; the
    first 5 are a flat $15;
  - annual: $30 per node a year (two months free) for a committed count of at least 5; nodes
    above it are billed monthly at $3.
- **Unchanged:** Cloud Free (1 cluster or fleet, 5 nodes, 3 days, 1 user), Pro's limits and
  30 days of history, how nodes are counted, and the Community edition: free, open source and
  without limits.
- **One source for the price in the product.** The plan page's estimate now reads the price
  from `GET /api/v1/plan`, as the plan strip and the upgrade page already did, so no page
  writes an amount of its own.
- **The billing notice says what Pro costs.** The email that goes out 14 days before billing
  starts for an organization on the beta's free Pro now states the price.
- **The Cloud docs** describe open sign-up (no invite, no waiting list) and how paying for Pro
  works ([WatchLog Cloud](https://github.com/watchlog-light/watchlog/blob/v0.9.3/docs/site/cloud.md#paying-for-pro)).

## Fixed

- The email about reaching Cloud Free's node limit said Pro was free during the beta, whether
  or not it still was. It now points to the plan page.

## Upgrading

No manual steps and no database migration. Agents are unchanged: 0.9.2 agents need no
upgrade.
