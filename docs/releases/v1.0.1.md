# WatchLog 1.0.1: a whole Prometheus through one token, and a stacked chart that fits

A patch release for Your metrics.

## What's changed

- **Remote write is limited by your organisation's series, not as one source**
  ([docs](https://github.com/watchlog-light/watchlog/blob/v1.0.1/docs/site/your-metrics.md#what-each-plan-includes),
  ADR-240). In 1.0.0 a remote-write token counted as one source and was cut at 500 series (100
  on Cloud Free), so a Prometheus that sent 5,000 kept 500. A token's remote write now has no
  cap of its own: it brings as many series as your plan has (200 on Cloud Free; 200 for each
  billed node or server on Cloud Pro, at least 1,000; your installation's setting when
  self-hosted). A scraped endpoint and a token's pushes keep their cap, the rule of 100 values
  for one label still refuses a metric by the label's name, and when your plan's series are
  full, new series are refused while known ones keep flowing. Nothing to change on your side:
  a token that was cut at 500 is admitted up to your plan from its next request.

## What's fixed

- **A split drawn as areas or bars stays inside its chart.** A widget or a metric split by a
  label and drawn as stacked areas (or stacked bars) scaled its axis to the largest series, so
  the series stacked above it were cut off at the top. The axis now holds their sum.

## Tested this time

- A watched metric through What changed, hour by hour and minute by minute on generated data:
  learnt, scored, on the live map and in the story under its workload or server, with its
  "outside its usual range" alert firing and resolving, on a Kubernetes cluster and on a Server
  fleet. The story connects it to what its workload calls on the service map (on a fleet, to a
  container of its server). No fix was needed.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do; no migration. Agents and collectors need no update.
