# WatchLog 0.12.0: a light theme

The dashboard now comes in light as well as dark, and follows your computer's setting by
default.

## What's new

- **Theme in the account menu:** System, Light or Dark, three buttons on the Theme row of the
  menu under your name. System follows your computer's setting and changes with it; Light and
  Dark stay. Dark looks exactly as before.
- **Saved on your user:** the choice follows you to another browser, and the page opens in it
  without a flash of the other theme. The live demo keeps it in your browser.
- **Every page in both themes,** Kubernetes and Servers alike: status text, links, charts and
  the service map use colours that read on white, and charts redraw when the theme changes.
- **API:** `theme` on `GET /api/v1/me`; `PATCH /api/v1/me/preferences` with `{"theme": "light"}`
  (a signed-in session only).

## Upgrading

No manual steps; a migration adds the user's theme (System for everyone). Agents don't need
an update for this release. Behind a strict Content-Security-Policy of your own, allow the
one inline script in `index.html` by its hash, as WatchLog Cloud's policy does (see
`backend/src/lib/csp.ts`).
