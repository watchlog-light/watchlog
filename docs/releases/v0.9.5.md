# WatchLog 0.9.5

**WatchLog Cloud no longer says Pro is free when it isn't.** Billing is live on WatchLog
Cloud, but several messages still offered "Pro, free during the beta". Every place that offers
Pro now makes the same offer: the upgrade, at the price from the plans config
([ADR-205, amended](https://github.com/watchlog-light/watchlog/blob/v0.9.5/docs/DECISIONS.md)).
Nothing changes for self-hosted Community installs, which have no plans.

## Fixed

- **The plan-limit messages** of Add cluster, the onboarding wizard and invites said "Try Pro,
  free during the beta". They now name the limit and offer the upgrade with its price:
  "Upgrade to Pro in Settings › Plan & billing: $3 per node or server a month, $15 minimum."
- **The over-limit banner's link** and **the first week's "invite a teammate" step** said the
  same. They now read "Upgrade to Pro · $3 per node →" and the sentence above.
- **The email to Owners when a limit is hit** said "try Pro (free during the beta)". It now
  states Pro's price, as the Free-limit email does since 0.9.3.
- **The plan page** showed "WatchLog Cloud is in beta. Pro is free until billing starts"
  whenever it had no answer from the billing service, and the plan strip said "Try Pro, free
  during beta" in the same case. Both now say so only when the billing service itself answers
  that billing is off.
- **An organization that already pays** and reaches a limit is no longer told to try Pro.
- **The API's `plan-limit` refusals** (409 and 402) say "Upgrade to Pro" where they said "Try
  Pro".

## Unchanged

A Cloud installation that runs with billing off (`BILLING_LIVE=false`) still offers Pro free
during its beta, and says so on the pages that only appear then. Organizations that started
Pro during the beta still see "free during the beta, until <date>".

## Upgrading

No manual steps and no database migration. Agents are unchanged: 0.9.2 agents need no
upgrade.
