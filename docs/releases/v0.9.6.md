# WatchLog 0.9.6

**Two fixes for WatchLog Cloud, found on Cloud itself.** What changed now counts 7 days of
_data_ before it lists anything, by one rule that its worker and its learning page share, and
an organization that staff move to Pro gets Pro's limits
([ADR-217](https://github.com/watchlog-light/watchlog/blob/v0.9.6/docs/DECISIONS.md)).
Nothing changes for self-hosted Community installs.

## Fixed

- **The learning page's top and bottom disagreed after a gap.** The day, the ring and "Ready
  on" counted 7 days from the first time WatchLog saw the organization on Pro; the bar, the
  tile and the grid showed the hours really learned. An organization that had Pro for two
  hours, lost it for two days and got it back read "day 3 of 7, ready on Monday" over "0 of
  168". Everything on the page now comes from the worker's own state:
  - the day is one for every 24 hours of the week learned;
  - the ring shows the week as learned, so a day that was not learned stays empty;
  - "Ready on" is when the worker will start listing, by the rule it scores with.
- **The worker itself turned the list on 7 days after the first sighting,** even when days in
  between were never learned. It now waits until every hour of the week has been learned
  since a series was first seen. Without a gap that is exactly 7 days, as before; with one,
  the missing hours are learned when they come round a week later.
- **Coming back within 30 days:** what was learned before still counts (the baselines are
  kept and used), the page says "Learning resumed on <date>", and a cluster that had finished
  learning lists again at once. After 30 days learning starts over at day 1.
- **The same numbers everywhere:** the sidebar's "day N/7", the alert card's line, the API's
  `learning` (new: `resumedAt`; `until` is the worker's estimate), MCP `get_what_changed`, and
  the "What changed is ready" email, which goes out when the worker says ready.
- **An organization staff moved from Free to Pro kept Free's limits** (1 cluster, 5 nodes, 1
  user, 3 days of data), because the admin form sent the limits it had. A plan change now
  takes the new plan's limits unless a limit is a number of the organization's own; existing
  rows are repaired by a migration. Paid organizations were not affected.
- **"Unlimited" typed for a limit in the admin API became 0.** It stays unlimited.

## Upgrading

No manual steps. Two database migrations run by themselves (four columns for What changed's
learning row; the repair of staff-set Pro rows left with Free's limits). Agents are unchanged:
0.9.2 agents need no upgrade. In WatchLog Cloud, a cluster that is learning shows its corrected
day and date as soon as the update is running.
