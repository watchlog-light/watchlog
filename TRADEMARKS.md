# Trademarks

The WatchLog name and the WatchLog logo are trademarks of the WatchLog maintainer. They are
not covered by the code's licences.

The [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE) lets you use, change and share the code. It doesn't give you
the right to use the name or the logo (AGPL-3.0 section 7(e)). This policy says what you can
do with them.

## Fine without asking

- **Saying what your work is built on or works with**, accurately: "works with WatchLog",
  "a Helm chart for WatchLog", "based on WatchLog", "a fork of WatchLog".
- **Talking about WatchLog:** articles, talks, tutorials, reviews and comparisons.
- **Running WatchLog unchanged**, for yourself or your organisation, under its own name.
- **Sharing unchanged releases** with the name and logo as they are.

When you use the name like this, don't make it look like the maintainer made, endorses or
supports your product or service.

## Needs a different name and logo

- **Forks and changed versions you distribute.** If you share a changed build, publish an
  image or run a public service from changed code, give it your own name and logo. The
  name must not be WatchLog or easily mistaken for it (for example "WatchLog Pro" or
  "WatchLogs"). Keep the licence and copyright notices, and you may say it is based on
  WatchLog.
- **Products and services that aren't WatchLog**, such as a hosted offering, a plugin or
  a company name. These can't use the name or the logo as their own.
- **Domain names, package names and social accounts** that use the name in a way that
  could be mistaken for the project's own.

To do this with a fork, change the name in the UI, the image and chart names, and the
logo. The logo is in `packages/design-tokens/assets/` and the name in
`web/src/i18n/en.json`.

## Questions

If you're unsure whether a use is fine, or want permission for one not listed here, ask
at hello@watchlog.io.
