Your first alert, explained

WatchLog just sent the first alert for acme. How to read it:

- Its facts say where, since when, which rule, and the value against its
  limit.
- “Likely cause” appears when WatchLog found one cause clearly more
  likely than the others. The alert’s page shows the evidence and the
  other possible causes.
- You get another email when it resolves, and reminders while it keeps
  firing.

Too noisy? Each rule’s threshold, duration and channels can be changed
on the Alerts page, and a silence mutes an alert for a while.
